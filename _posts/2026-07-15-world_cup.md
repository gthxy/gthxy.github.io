I had the incredible opportunity to volunteer at the **2026 FIFA World Cup**, working in both **Catering** and **Fan Operations**.

Over the course of the tournament, I volunteered at five matches:

- **June 18:** South Africa vs. Czech Republic
- **June 21:** Spain vs. Saudi Arabia
- **July 1:** England vs. DR Congo
- **July 7:** Argentina vs. Egypt
- **July 15:** Argentina vs. England — **Semifinal**

Experiencing the World Cup from behind the scenes was completely different from watching it as a fan. From helping with stadium operations to seeing supporters from around the world come together, it was one of the most memorable experiences of my time in the US. :soccer:
