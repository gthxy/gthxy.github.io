Xinyue Huang

黄欣悦 • Living young and wild and free

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar at the California Institute of Technology (Caltech), working in Daraio Research Group on next-generation sensing and wearable technologies in collaboration with Meta.

I received my PhD in Biomedical Engineering from the Georgia Institute of Technology and Emory University, where I worked in the Ultrasound Imaging and Therapeutics Research Lab under the guidance of Dr. Stanislav Emelianov. During my PhD, I also earned an MS in Computer Science from Georgia Tech, specializing in Machine Learning, and interned with the Sensors Team at Meta Reality Labs.

My research focuses on developing ultrasound-based imaging and wearable sensing technologies, spanning ultrasound, photoacoustic, elasticity, and Doppler imaging. believe ultrasound is a versatile sensing tool capable of revealing far more information than conventionally recognized.

Before joining GT/Emory, I completed my undergraduate studies in Biomedical Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, where I was advised by Dr. Puxiang Lai. I then earned my master’s degree in Biomedical Engineering from Johns Hopkins University, co-advised by Dr. Kai Ding and Dr. Muyinatu Bell.

I am originally from China, born in the south and raised in the northeast. There is far more to me than a biography can capture, getting to know me will tell you the rest!
